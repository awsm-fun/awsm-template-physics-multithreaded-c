Multithreaded Physics Demo

A copyable template from the Awsm project — a WebGPU game skeleton running across three threads, all over one shared WebAssembly.Memory: a C app that owns the whole game and steps Box3D on its own worker threads, a Rust renderer, and a plain JavaScript page that takes the input and plays the sound. No postMessage on any hot path, real atomics, wasm SIMD in the solver.

  • Rendering: AwsmRenderer
  • Audio: AwsmAudio
  • Physics: Box3D

Roll the red ball with WASD, jump with Space, click the table to drop more balls (R clears them) — every impact is synthesized and spatialized in real time from the physics contacts. Drag with the right mouse button to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom; the controls and the stereo image stay relative to your view from any angle.

The stats panel (the Stats button, bottom left) shows each thread live: the renderer's frame rate and its GPU and CPU time per frame, the app's CPU time per physics step, and whether the picture keeps in step with the simulation. Box3D runs its solver on the app's thread plus up to four more (Emscripten pthreads sharing the app's memory, scheduled by Box3D itself). Drop more balls and watch the physics time grow.
